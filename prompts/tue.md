## Tuesday waiver report (for week {week})
Goal: tell Dan which waiver claims to submit before claims process, with FAAB bids.

1. Find the holes in Dan's week {week} lineup: starters on bye (get_schedule byes), players Out or on IR (get_injury_report, statuses), and weak spots.
2. Find candidates: get_free_agents for each position of need, and get_trending_players (add, last 24-48 hours) to see who is rising. In this 12-team dynasty league most trending players are already rostered: skip a trending player who isn't in get_free_agents results, unless get_free_agents failed or doesn't cover his position. Check each candidate's usage (get_player_usage) and week {week} matchup.
3. If Dan has no healthy, non-bye QB for week {week}, the top recommendation MUST be a QB streamer.
   Look one week ahead too (get_schedule for next week): if a starting slot will have no healthy, non-bye player next week, say so. If that fill-in should be claimed now (good options are scarce, or other teams share the bye and will want him), make it claim #1.
4. For each add, name the drop (or an IR move that frees a spot) on the claim's first line, e.g. "Add X (WR PIT) $3, drop Y." Dan has 25 roster spots plus 3 IR (league.json): count his non-IR players in get_my_roster, and give each open spot to one claim with no drop ("Add X (WR PIT) $3, open spot."). Pick drops by least long-term dynasty value AND least 2026 help. Prefer IR moves over drops when a player qualifies. Every claim needs its own spot: never name the same drop or IR move for two claims, since Yahoo rejects a claim whose drop is already gone.
5. Bids: a whole-dollar FAAB bid per claim from Dan's remaining budget. Take it from whichever is newer (compare as_of): faab_remaining in get_my_roster, or dan_remaining in get_league_context's faab_market; ignore get_my_roster's figure when it carries a warning that it's the seeded or an old copy. league.json only has the starting budget. Price each bid from what this league actually pays: faab_market lists recent winning bids, price ranges by position (top and median), claims Dan lost, and each team's budget left (with Yahoo working, get_transactions also shows this week's bids).
   - Must-haves: a claim that fills an otherwise empty starting slot (this week or next), or adds a likely weekly starter for the rest of 2026, gets a bid that beats the top recent price for a comparable player (same position and role). Losing him costs more than a few extra dollars.
   - Players Dan wants who aren't sure starters (a rising role, a breakout, a high-upside handcuff): bid around the median price for that position, and above it when a team with money left that bids often is likely to want him.
   - Pure depth and dart throws: $0-3.
   - In the claim's reasons, name the comparable, e.g. "a starting QB went for $13 last week."
   - Keep the reserve from the strategy, except for a claim that fills an otherwise empty starting slot. If faab_market has no data, say the bids weren't checked against this league's prices.
6. Rank up to 5 claims by how much each matters, slot-fillers first. Also give the projected starting lineup for week {week} as it would look after the top claim.
